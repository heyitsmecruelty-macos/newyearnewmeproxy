Make the numbers make sense.

A quick landing pad for the building blocks of math. Start with the idea, connect it to a pattern, then try it on your own.

Core ideas

01 / ALGEBRA

Find the unknown

An equation stays balanced when you do the same operation to both sides. Undo operations in reverse order to isolate a variable.

02 / GEOMETRY

See the structure

Angles in a triangle add to 180°. Area measures the space inside a shape; perimeter measures the distance around it.

03 / FUNCTIONS

Spot the relationship

A function maps each input to one output. Tables, graphs, and rules are different ways to describe the same relationship.

Quick practice

Solve for x: 3x + 5 = 20

Subtract 5 from both sides, then divide both sides by 3. Check by substituting your answer into the original equation.

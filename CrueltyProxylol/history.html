Understand the past in context.

History is more than dates. Connect causes, choices, and consequences to see how people and events shaped the world around them.

Ways to connect the story

01 / TIMELINE

What happened when?

Put events in sequence, then notice what changed quickly and what took generations to unfold.

02 / CAUSE & EFFECT

Why did it happen?

Separate long-term conditions from immediate triggers. Most major events have several causes, not just one.

03 / PERSPECTIVE

Whose account is it?

Compare sources, consider who created them and when, and look for voices that are missing from the record.

Quick practice

Build a three-part event summary

Write one sentence for the conditions before an event, one for the turning point, and one for its lasting effects.
